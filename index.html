The salon

Expertise, in a room that feels like your own.

Hair by Kelly was built around one idea: that great hair should feel effortless to live with, not just beautiful in the mirror on the day. Every colour, cut and treatment is planned around your hair’s condition, your routine and the way you actually want to feel — confident, put-together, unmistakably you.

Alongside our hair services, we offer a considered range of beauty treatments — from bridal makeup to facials and threading — so a single visit can carry you from an ordinary Tuesday to the biggest day on your calendar.
